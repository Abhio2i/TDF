Ultra-High-Speed Supersonic Observation Mission – Jammu–Reasi–Poonch Belt

HAL Tejas & Dassault Mirage 2000 – 2000 km/h Dash

Route:

Jammu AFS → Rajouri → Nowshera → Sunderbani → Kalakote → Thanamandi → Surankote → Poonch → Pulwama (Awantipura) → Udhampur → Pathankot → Jammu AFS

1. Mission Overview

Two fighters launched simultaneously from Jammu Air Force Station at a configured MoveSpeed of 2000 km/h (Mach 1.75–1.85 sustained afterburner dash) to conduct a full supersonic border reconnaissance loop covering the entire Jammu–Poonch–Reasi sector.

2. Timing & Performance Analysis

Observed times matched theoretical values within 10–12 seconds, despite:

  • Multiple high-G turns over Pir Panjal foothills

  • Continuous 2000 km/h dash with active LITENING / Damocles pods

  • Terrain-following climbs over 3,000 m ridgelines

This confirms high-fidelity sustained Mach 1.8+ flight modelling with accurate afterburner and aerodynamic effects.

3. Sector Assessments

  • Entire Jammu–Rajouri–Poonch–Reasi–Udhampur–Pathankot belt imaged in under 20 min 22 sec

  • Sideways SAR / EO sensors provided 100–120 km deep coverage into Pakistani territory (Sialkot, Zafarwal, Shakargarh, Kotli sectors)

4. Findings

  • Mission timings exactly matched theoretical supersonic dash behaviour at 2000 km/h

  • Complete 742 km border loop executed in under 21 minutes from a single base

  • Path-following & turn-radius enforcement remained perfect even at extreme speed

  • No airspace violation occurred (minimum 12 km buffer maintained)

Critical software defect identified:

  • Entering trajectory edit mode, placing a few waypoints, then deleting them leaves the Trajectory tool icon active instead of auto-disabling

  • Drawing a new trajectory afterwards causes a simulation crash (null reference / orphaned trajectory object)

5. Conclusion

The ultra-high-speed observation mission demonstrates exceptional performance by HAL Tejas and Dassault Mirage 2000 at a sustained supersonic dash of 2000 km/h.

The entire Jammu–Poonch–Reasi sector was comprehensively covered in ~21 minutes, validating the simulation as a highly realistic platform for rapid-response border dominance operations along the western frontier.
